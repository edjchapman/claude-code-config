# Content Style

Prefer **concise, learnable, referenceable** over complete. A document earns its length by
what a reader can _act on_, not by what it covers.

## Length

- **Distil, don't accumulate.** When refreshing a document, cut at least as much as you add.
  Length that survives a rewrite unexamined is length nobody chose.
- **One artefact, one job.** A sheet read under pressure and a reference read at leisure are
  different documents. Split them rather than serving both badly.

## Structure

- **Front-load the answer.** What to do goes first; why goes after it, or below it.
- **Scannable by default:** short bullets, bolded anchors, tables where the data is tabular.
  A section that can't be skimmed in ten seconds needs a heading, a cut, or both.
- Prose walls, restated context and hedging preamble are the standing failure mode — delete
  them on sight, including in text written earlier in the same session.

## Speaking

- **Speakable beats comprehensive** for anything performed out loud — interview answers,
  standups, demos, incident comms. Write the words to say, not a description of them.
